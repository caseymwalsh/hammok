What matters for you

  • Width. In a hammock the sides wrap around you, so a 50″ quilt is usually enough; Hammock Gear recommends slim for hammock use. On the ground, a broad sleeper wants 55″ or more so the edges reach the pad.
  • Length. At 5′7″, short or regular is right. Short saves an ounce or two and a little money.
  • Footbox. A zippered footbox opens flat as a blanket on warm nights; a sewn one is warmer and slightly lighter. Either works in a hammock.
  • Ratings line up with the Incubator. A 20°F top quilt with your 20°F underquilt is a matched pair. A 30°F top quilt plus warm clothes also covers most of spring and fall.
  • Packing. Only 40°F quilts fit in the Big Pig alongside the hammock and Incubator. A 20°F top quilt goes in its own stuff sack or loose in the dry bag.
  • Pad straps and draft collars matter more on the ground than in a hammock. Skip them for hammock-only use to save money.
