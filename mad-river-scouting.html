🏕️ Mad River State Forest — Friday, October 23, 2026 Completed

Day trip · Scouting for hammock sites · Town of Florence, Oneida County

GoalDay trip, no overnight — find good hammock camping spots for a future trip — log at least one site in the after-action report below.

WeatherPartly sunny and dry. High around 55°F, morning low near 32°F — frost likely early, ground and leaves wet until it burns off. 10% chance of rain. Sunrise ~7:25 AM, sunset ~6:10 PM, so about 10¾ hours of daylight. (Forecast as of Thu Oct 22.)

Heads upDeer hunting season is open; the forest is used for hunting, so wear something bright.

The forest2,933 acres of rolling terrain with streams and wetlands; mix of conifer plantations (pine, larch, spruce, Douglas fir) and natural hardwood/hemlock. No maintained trails — about 7.7 miles of paved and unpaved roads cross the property.

AccessFrom Camden, Route 13 north ~0.6 mi, right on River Road ~3.1 mi. Phelan Road parking area: 43.40976, -75.75749.

ParkedOct 23: 43.40479, -75.75745 (Plus Code C63V+W27), about 550 m south of the DEC Phelan Road parking area.

Camping rulesPrimitive camping allowed; sites must be 150+ ft from any road, trail, or water. Permit needed for 3+ nights or groups of 10+. Dead and down wood only for fires.

ContactsDEC Region 6 Herkimer office (315) 866-6330 · Emergencies: 911 or 1-833-NYS-RANGERS · DEC page · GeoPDF map
