Trip Debrief

Weather
Forecast held: Saturday was cool and dry as predicted, rain moved in Sunday during the hike out.
Sleep & warmth
Not warm enough overnight. Underquilt helped but wasn't sufficient on its own — ended up in a sweater, hiking pants, and eventually long underwear. A top quilt is a real need, not just a nice-to-have.
Bug net
Did its job but was genuinely annoying to deal with. An integrated bug net on the next hammock is now a non-negotiable.
Kootek hammock
Slept in it, wasn't bad, but diagonal lay technique didn't click — possibly a length issue with this model. Worth investigating before ruling the hammock itself out.
Hydration
Sawyer Squeeze didn't arrive in time for this trip. Nalgene + HydroJug covered hydration needs fine without it.
Backpack rain cover
Carried but not deployed — didn't look like it would fit over the bag once fully packed.
Fire
Two fires made: one lit in a single strike with a match the instructor provided (not part of the carried kit), one started with the ferro rod.
Hemostat
Used — but for pulling a sausage out of the fire, not first aid. Trauma shears rode along unused.
Bug spray
Definitely needed. The 6 oz OFF! Deep Woods volume was the right amount.
Unused but not dead weight
Whistle, hand warmers, and the fishing kit went unused this trip, but aren't considered worth cutting.
Not on the original list
Toilet paper and Excedrin were grabbed last-minute and carried, unlogged until now.
The backpack itself
Miserable to carry. A different pack is needed — this is the clearest actionable item from the trip.
